**Project Overview**
This project analyses a simulated Zepto financial data to evaluate business performance, revenue trends, product profitability, operational expenses and customer payment behaviour.
**Key Business Insights**
_Revenue Performance: Generated approximately ₹3.55 lakh in net revenue.
Profitability Analysis: Achieved approximately ₹86,974 gross profit and ₹44,098 contribution profit.
Payment Analysis: UPI was the leading payment method, contributing approximately 54.8% of net revenue.
Refund Analysis: Identified 297 refunded transaction records, with approximately ₹23,279 in refunds.
Product Analysis: Laundry Detergent was the highest-revenue product.
Profitability Improvement: Staples recorded the lowest category contribution.__
